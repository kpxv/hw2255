The testbench passes.

\begin{enumerate}
	\item The maximum observed delay is approximately 25 ns.
	\item Waits on an XOR and two ANDs for the first bit. Waits on an
	      additional two AND gates for every bit after. Thus,
          $(3 + 1 + 1) + 15 \cdot (1 + 1) = 35$ ns until the last carry-out is
          trusted. There is one XOR gate after the second-to-last carry-in to
          determine the final sum bit, which means the final sum bit is 36 ns.
          This design is about 30\% faster.
\end{enumerate}
